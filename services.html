Questions

05 answers
Do I need my content ready before we start?+

No. I’ll send a simple outline of what each page needs and help shape the wording. If you don’t have photos, we can plan a short shoot or choose quality stock images together.

Where will the site be hosted, and what does it cost?+

On a fast, reliable host under your own account, usually between $0 and $20 a month depending on traffic. Hosting is included if you choose the care plan.

Can I make changes myself after launch?+

Yes, for everyday updates like text, hours, and photos. I’ll show you how during handover. Larger changes are covered by the care plan or quoted separately.

I already have a website. Can you work with it?+

Yes, that’s what the Redesign package is for. I start by auditing what’s there, keep what works, and set up redirects so you don’t lose your search ranking.

How does payment work?+

Half to start and half at launch. Projects over $500 can be split into three payments, and I’m happy to talk through a payment plan if that makes it possible. Everything is agreed in writing before work begins.

Tell me about your business.

Start with a free 20-minute call. I’ll ask what you do, who your customers are, and what the site needs to do for you.
